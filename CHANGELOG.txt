Parsec — changelog
The version notes that used to sit in the code, newest first. Parsec 8 took them out of the file; Parsec 11.1 is the newest.

Parsec 11.1 — the Technician page's RO in your layout
  · The RO on the Technician page is in two columns now, as you arranged it on the RO page mock-up. In the middle: Operations, Parts on this RO, Activity & notes, Fault codes, Photos & codes, Messages about this RO, Truck history, Warranty claim, Customer, Estimate & approval, Ready to close. At the right: Truck & status, Stage bar, Who has it, People, Schedule, Vehicle, Money. Next step and Paperwork are off
  · The right column stays in view while the middle scrolls, so the truck, its status and Done, Stop, Hold, Switch and Ask the foreman are always there. On a narrow window or a phone it's one column: the truck first, then the work, then the facts
  · An open operation is laid out as on the RO page: its codes, the past fix (Fixed N× before opens it, with Use fix) and its status; the clock card (the time on it, start or stop, quoted, burnt, left, efficiency); and the tabs Time, Parts and Files
  · Notes moved into the Time tab: every punch shows its start, the notes written during it, then its stop and how long it ran. Cause, correction and notes written off the clock sit where their time is. The note box under it writes into the punch that's running
  · Files tab: the files on that operation — add pictures or PDFs, and open one right on the page
  · New blocks, on the shop's real data: Parts on this RO (every part on every operation, Picked up), Activity & notes (what happened, and a note on the operation), Fault codes (add one — 3226/2 or P0401 — or take one off), Messages about this RO (the Inbox notes about it, and send one to the foreman, Parts or the advisor), Truck history (earlier visits for the same VIN, flagged when it's the same problem), Warranty claim (only when the RO has warranty work), Customer (their other open ROs, the last contact), Estimate & approval (each operation's price, approved or not, Ask the advisor), Ready to close (the four close-out checks), Who has it (who it waits on, how long, Nudge)
  · People, Schedule, Vehicle and Money fold up with their headings
  · Your day is no longer on the RO pane (it wasn't in your layout) — the header still shows the clock and efficiency, and the Time Log has the day

Parsec 11 — the Technician page
  · A new page right below Inbox, for technicians only: your repair orders in four groups — Working, Assigned (in the foreman's order, paused ones first), Waiting (with how long each has waited, red when it's past the follow-up time) and Done today
  · The list on the left (the truck, the RO, the customer, the complaint, the clock or the hours), the RO on the right: the truck band with its status and, while you work, the running clock; Done, Stop, Hold, Switch, Ask the foreman; Start, Resume or Bring it in; Ask Parts; Open RO; the stage bar
  · Every operation opens to its notes (type and press Enter — the time goes in), its time (quoted, burnt, left, efficiency, and every punch: the day, start and stop, how long, why it started and stopped) and its parts (Picked up, Ask Parts for a part). The past fix shows inside the operation, with View and Use fix. Open all / Fold all
  · Search your ROs by number, customer, truck, year, unit or complaint (/ to jump there). J and K move, Enter starts, S stops, Space starts or stops, Ctrl Enter finishes, H holds
  · Everything Console does for a technician is here — Start my day, clock in and out, Scan, shop time, the Time Log fixes, photos and codes, Your day — so the Technician page replaces Console for technicians: it's their home page, and Console leaves their sidebar (the Manager can switch Console back on for anyone, Manager › Team). Foremen, managers and the rest keep Console as it was
  · Manager › Team shows the Technician page locked on for technicians, the way the Manager page is locked for managers
  · On a phone, technicians get it as their first tab ("My ROs"); the list opens each RO full screen
  · Notes sent from the Technician page still land in the Inbox the same way, and an Inbox note about Console opens the Technician page for a technician

Parsec 10.1 — the Manager page's Team opens on a new computer
  · Team did nothing when the computer had no ROs yet (a new computer or a new folder): every tab showed "No ROs yet". Team now opens anyway, and the empty page has a Set up the team button — people, roles, PINs and pay can go in before the first RO
  · Team also did nothing when nobody was on the team yet (the page stopped on an empty list). It now says no one is on the team yet, with Add a person
  · Everything else is unchanged from 10

Parsec 9.5 — the Manager page opens again
  · The Manager page wouldn't open ("RangeError: Invalid string length" in the console) when nobody had clock time in the last 14 workdays: the hours chart's scale came out 0 and its grid lines never stopped. The scale now starts at 10 hours, and every chart on the page checks its steps
  · Causes and corrections set by "Use fix" or from the phone were wiped each time Parsec opened again. They now go into the operation's work log and stay. Sample history that had lost them shows them again (Warranty shows more claims ready to file, the Console shows matching past fixes)
  · Manager no longer stops on a team member saved without a name, or a broken line in the activity log
  · The phone gets the cause and correction as the RO shows them (typed field plus the work log)

Parsec Mobile 3 — the phone app for technicians (tabs at the bottom)
  · Three places: Work, Inbox, Fixes. Triage, Foreman, Manager, Parts, the RO list and Settings stay on the computer
  · Work: start of day (your order, the foreman's note), the day clock with air and efficiency, On it now / Next / Waiting / Done today
  · A job: start, stop and switch the clock; status (Switched job, Waiting – Parts / Approval / Foreman / Tech Support, Complete — needs the correction); notes (type, or hold the mic and talk); parts (Picked up, Installed, Need a part); files by Dash pic / Pictures / DTCs / Other; cause and correction
  · Clock out: Lunch, Appointment or break, End of day — End of day first shows a job still running, air with no job, and jobs with no note today. Works on a day clock started on the computer too
  · Inbox: for you / done, open the job or mark it done. Fixes: for your jobs first, search, "Use on …" adds the cause, correction and missing parts — only when you tap it
  · Sync by QR both ways. "Photos later" sends everything else in a few seconds and keeps the photos for another sync
  · Photos: the full one stays on the phone (share it from your initials); a small copy goes by QR — 512 pixels, DTC pictures 900 so the codes stay readable
  · Works with no signal once opened from its https address; nothing is sent anywhere except by QR
  · Measured: changes alone 1–3 s. A plain photo about 3–5 s. A busy photo is about 120 codes — 36 s a loop, about 45–70 s on average when the webcam misses some codes

Parsec 9.4 — Parsec Mobile 3 sync (still QR codes, no network)
  · Send to phone carries one technician's work: their ROs (assigned, sent to them, or worked in the last 7 days), today's clock and punches, their Inbox, the foreman's note and the Fixes for their jobs. "Whose phone" picks the tech
  · Get from phone takes the day clock (in, lunch, break, end of day), clock time — a job still running at the sync arrives up to that moment and grows on the next sync, never twice — Picked up and Installed, photos (filed on the RO under Dash pic, Pictures, DTCs or Other), Inbox items done, notes, part requests, cause, correction and status
  · Spare codes: after every 6 codes comes a spare that fills in any one the webcam missed, so one miss no longer costs a whole loop. The webcam reads at full size. Older phone versions skip the spares and still work
  · Phone time that overlaps time already on the computer for that tech is left out with a message (fix it in the Time Log); time on a closed RO is left out, like before
  · A photo is only cleared from the phone once the computer has stored it
  · After Get from phone the message says what came (clock time, the day clock, photos, notes …)
  · Where it is: your initials (bottom left) › Your data › Parsec Mobile

Parsec 9.3 — the shop folder from anywhere, the shop Day report
  · The folder error: "Couldn't connect that folder: SecurityError" came from opening Parsec inside a preview (like the file preview in the Claude app). A preview can't reach folders on the computer, so the browser refuses. Opened in Chrome or Edge on its own, picking works
  · The folder screen now says so: inside a preview it tells you to open the file in Chrome or Edge, and lets you look around without a folder
  · Picking starts on the Desktop. Any folder works — make one on the Desktop (like "Parsec Shop") and pick it. The Desktop, Documents and Downloads themselves can't be picked (the browser blocks whole system folders)
  · Parsec checks it can save in the folder before using it. Everything goes there: Shop (people, settings, Inbox, timesheets, activity log), one folder per RO (the RO page with its punches, and its Attachments), and a daily backup
  · Problems in plain words on the folder screen: no folder picked, the browser said no, the folder was moved or deleted, the folder is read-only — with the browser's own words underneath
  · Parsec never opens the picker by itself anymore (a folder that's gone used to try, and the browser refused); it waits for your click
  · The folder screen and the sign-in screen sit in the middle of the window (they were pushed right)
  · Day report: the whole shop on one sheet (mock-up option C) — totals, a line per tech with their day, jobs worked by RO, what to look at, signatures. Arrows or the date to move between days; a tech sees only their own line
  · Print: the Day report and the Timesheet print on a clean page of their own — only the sheet, the colours kept, a long sheet runs onto a second page. Save as PDF from the print window
  · The End-of-day sheet pop-up is gone — the Time Log's Shop view and its print menu open the Day report

Parsec 9.1 — your answers to "things I need from you"
  · The shop folder comes first: when Parsec opens it asks for the shared folder (or to open it again), and everything — ROs, people, Inbox, timesheets, Career notes, Manager settings, Parts returns — is saved there. "Just this computer for now" is there for a day the folder can't be reached
  · Manager → Settings (the gear): labor rate ($210 an hour), pay period (weekly, every two weeks, monthly, and the day it starts), monthly overhead, the Admin PIN, the data folder
  · Manager → Team: add and remove people, role, pages they can open, PIN, and hourly pay per person
  · Career's pay estimate is hourly: hours on the clock × the rate the manager entered, time and a half over 40 h a week, on the manager's pay period
  · Time Log timesheets follow the pay period setting — weekly, every two weeks or monthly, with a total at the end of each week
  · Parts: part cost is typed at check-in, on a new part, or on the catalog item; gross profit, margins and absorption on the Manager page use what's entered (no more sample numbers)
  · Fixed: setting a KPI target and the KPI search box on the Manager page
