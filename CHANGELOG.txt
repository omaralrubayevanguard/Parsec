Parsec — changelog
The version notes that used to sit in the code, newest first. Parsec 8 took them out of the file; Parsec 14.5 is the newest.

Parsec 14.5 — the RO page: every section folds from its heading
  · A click on a section’s heading folds it, and a click opens it again — Operations, Messages about this RO, Fault codes, Photos & codes, Parts on this RO, Truck history, Warranty claim, Customer, Estimate & approval, Ready to close, the stage and Who has it. Buttons in the heading (Open in Assets, Ask Parts, +, the pick box) still do their own thing
  · The chevron now sits at the far right of every heading, after the section’s own buttons, and is easy to see (it was faint, and on Customer and Estimate & approval it sat beside the title like a menu)
  · Pointing at a folded heading still opens it for a look; the truck card folds from its chevron only (its words and buttons are for clicking)

Parsec 14.1 — the RO flow: Done asks, comebacks reopen, one release list, Send from the Next bar · RO details editable on the RO list and Technician panes
  RO details, editable everywhere the RO opens:
  · The RO list’s pane and the Technician page’s right pane carry the same groups as the RO page — Vehicle (year, make, model, VIN, unit, mileage, engine hours, engine, engine serial, chassis, key tag, spot, department), Customer (name, cust #, contact, PO), People (writer, tech, foreman), Schedule (opened, promised, ready about, ETR) and Case (case #, auth, tech support case) — collapsible, click to change, Enter saves, Esc cancels, Tab moves to the next field
  · One Undo per change, an Activity line (RO 9481: Mileage “97,000 mi” → “512,345 mi”), the VIN upper-cased with a warning when it isn’t 17 characters; read-only without the Repair Orders page. One table drives the RO page’s fields and both panes, so they can’t drift
  The RO flow:
  · Done asks instead of refusing: an operation with no story or 0:00 clocked gets “Op 2 isn’t finished — finish it anyway?” with Write the story / Finish anyway; parts not installed gets Mark them installed and finish / Not yet (the same part status change, one Undo). The clock still stops as Hold tech until you answer
  · Release lists “Story on every operation” and “Hours · 0.0 h clocked of 2.7 h sold”; releasing with nothing clocked needs a note
  · Start on an operation ASIST still shows as Pending (no approval in Parsec) asks once: Start anyway / Wait. Activity logs “started before its approval”
  · Comebacks: a New RO — imported or typed — whose VIN (or unit + customer) was released within 90 days asks: Reopen RO 8877 as a comeback (recommended) / Open a new RO anyway. Reopen puts the new operations (marked COMEBACK) and parts on the old RO, reopens it on Hold tech and flags it as a comeback for Fixes, Manager and Assets; a new RO is tagged “comeback of 8877”. One Undo
  · The RO reads “Hold tech · 1 op waiting on parts” while any open operation can be worked; Waiting – Parts only when every open operation waits
  · One release list: the walk-around ticks sit inside the Release pop (the same ticks Truck checks shows), and Keys back / Truck clean aren’t asked again in Handover
  · Send to Foreman from the RO page’s Next bar sends straight away when there’s nothing to choose; the pop opens only when a tech has to be picked

Parsec 14 — Assets: every truck and its history · one status look on every page · the road test switch · every cost and the engine serial read · the RO flow fixes
  Assets (a new page, right after RO List):
  · Every truck the shop has worked on, one row per VIN (unit # and customer when there’s no VIN): picture, unit, year and model, customer, visits, last in. A flag with a count marks repeat repairs; a status icon marks a truck in the shop now
  · Chips for All, In the shop and Repeat repairs; sort by Last in, Most visits, Most days down or Customer A–Z; filter by customer. The search icon (or /) finds a VIN, unit, customer, model, engine serial or RO #. J/K move, Enter opens the truck’s open or latest RO
  · The truck: picture, year/make/model, VIN, unit, customer, engine, engine serial, miles, engine hours, last seen, and a button to its open RO. Lifetime tiles: visits, days down, labor and parts (money shows as — without “See costs and prices”)
  · Repeat repairs: the same system, complaint or part back within 90 days, or an operation marked Comeback — one line per visit and system
  · Miles and engine hours over time, with a monthly rate. Every visit, newest first: RO, status, miles and hours, techs, cost, and each operation with its job type, complaint, a Repeat tag, the fix and parts; a click opens the RO
  · In the sidebar and the phone’s More sheet; a page permission on for every role (the Admin page can turn it off). The RO page’s and Technician page’s Truck history have Open in Assets
  One status look:
  · RO, operation, part and warranty-claim statuses share one icon, color and words on every page — the RO page, Technician, Triage, Foreman, Parts, Inventory, Warranty, the RO list, Assets, Manager, Career, the Day report, the search windows and Ctrl K
  · To read: icon + word, no box (○ Hold tech, ◐ Working · Repairing, ◌ Waiting – Parts, ● Complete, ✓ Complete – Released). To change: an outlined pill with ▾, only where it opens a menu; without the permission you see the words
  · Parts: ○ Not ordered, ◔ Ordered, ! Backordered, ✓ Received · Staged · Picked up · Installed. “To order” is only the queue heading now. “No parts” is plain text, not a chip; parts waiting on approval say so
  · Gone: the tinted pills on Technician and Warranty, the square status dots on Triage and Foreman (the header shows the RO’s stage instead — “Approval · stage 3 of 7”, the same stage on every page), the grey part chips on Parts and Inventory
  The road test switch:
  · “Road test before release” under the truck card’s buttons on the RO page and the Technician page — on for every RO unless you turn it off (one Undo, an Activity line; it needs “Change an RO’s status”)
  · Off: no Road test or Truck checks buttons on any page, no Road test or Truck checks group in Needs action, no road-test or truck-check line in Ready to close or Release, the stage track and the RO list’s ticks skip Road test, Next step goes straight to “Release the truck”, and when the last operation is done the RO reads Complete, ready to release. Turning it back on brings everything back
  Reading from ASIST (New RO, Import, Extract operations, Inventory’s parts list):
  · Engine serial: Engine Serial / Serial Number / S/N / SN / ESN / Eng Serial # / Engine No. — from the page text, a screenshot or the PDF — lands in the RO’s Engine serial
  · Operation costs: labor hours, labor rate, labor $, parts $, shop supplies, sublet and the operation total — from the table’s columns, from “Labor Rate: / Parts: / Shop Supplies:” lines, or worked out from the sums. The preview shows the line (“Labor 6.2 h × $210.00/h = $1,302.00 · Parts · Shop supplies · Op total”) and whether it adds up; an operation’s own rate is kept
  · Part costs: List, Disc, Net/Unit, Core (per unit) and Ext; the part keeps them and the Parts list learns the price. The RO page’s Parts tab shows each part’s price; Money shows Core, Shop supplies and ASIST’s total
  · Totals: Labor, Parts, Core, Fees, Tax and Total are read and checked; tax is kept apart
  · ASIST’s approval becomes Parsec’s: Approved writes the approval (by ASIST, who created the case, when), Declined records the decline, Pending still needs approval; something approved or declined by hand in Parsec wins (“Kept yours”). Parts can order, Triage lists the RO, the RO list says the right next step
  · A “Parts” heading no longer sticks to the last operation’s name (old ones are fixed when the file opens); parts in a case-level block go to the operation ASIST names, or the one whose words match, never just the last one. The preview says where each went, with a dropdown to move the loose ones
  · Screenshot misreads fixed: $2 14.50, 1.302.00, RA0001241 190, E5N, DI3
  The RO flow, fixed:
  · An RO closes at Release, not at the road test — it stayed on the Open tab, the yard, tech loads and Manager’s counts; dateClosed is set at release; ROs auto-closed by a road test reopen when the file loads
  · With every operation done the RO reads Complete – Road test (or Complete), not Hold Tech “for want of a road test”; an approval on the operation counts as the customer’s approval
  · One Undo takes back a release: status, release record and closed date together
  · Notes, road test asks, go-aheads and parts-in sent to a technician show on their Technician page under Sent to you (with Done); the send toast says so
  · Next step: Waiting – Customer, then Send the estimate for unapproved operations, before any Order; only parts on approved or warranty operations count as to order; an approved operation with no tech reads “Assign a tech · Frank”
  · Check in finds parts that arrive early (every open order line; a folded “Not due yet — early” group; a scan says “early — due Fri”)
  · Automation toasts name the RO (“Automation · RO 8877 → Hold tech”), join a message shown in the last 3 s instead of replacing it, and don’t fire at load, during undo or for shop time
  · Days open counted the same way on the RO list, the RO page and Technician; the RO list’s Spot matches Foreman’s; Manager and Career count tiles show the plain change (▲ 23) instead of ▲ 1800%
  · The Foreman Assign pop’s bar matches its “7.4 h free”; an imported Location maps to a spot (UpTime 2 → UP, AW2 → Awning 2); a hand-typed New RO says “Created RO 0123” (from ASIST only when it was); “No tech yet” instead of “with T Technician”; the release toast names what’s still open properly
  · Without the permission: a Technician sees the RO status as words, and the Parts page is read-only (no Order…, Chase…, Check in, Stage it, Send to)
  · Off-site requests: no more randomuser.me portraits (people show initials or their own photo) and the hidden assistant’s call to api.anthropic.com is gone. Google Fonts still loads at start; pdf.js is fetched only when someone reads a PDF (code only — the PDF stays here)

Parsec 13 — the login page, switching who’s signed in, and the Admin page: people and permissions
  The login page:
  · Shows when someone signs out, switches, or the app opens. On the left: Parsec, the shop, the time and date big, and the clocks that are running — whose, which RO, since when ("Signing in or out never stops one")
  · On the right: Who’s signing in? — Recent on this computer first, then each department. Each name has a dot when they’re on the clock (green on a job, amber on the day clock) and a line under it (on the clock · RO 1356, on the day clock, 3 open ROs, or the role). Admin is a small link at the bottom; people whose sign-in is off aren’t shown
  · A 4-digit PIN for everyone, on a number pad (keys work too). Someone with no PIN yet, or a temporary one, picks their own — typed twice — and they’re in. The Admin sets a PIN the first time
  · A wrong PIN: five tries, then a minute’s wait, with a countdown
  · PINs are kept scrambled in the file, never as typed; old plain PINs were scrambled when the file opened
  Switching and stepping away:
  · Switch user is a quick switcher off your name: recent people and Someone else; pick one and their PIN pad opens over the page. Your clock keeps running
  · Step away — lock it yourself (in the same menu, or Ctrl L): your page stays where it was, behind your PIN; a refresh doesn’t get past it; "Not Mike? Switch user"
  · Everyone signed in is signed out at the end of the shop day (running clocks keep running). No lock when nobody’s touched it, unless the Admin page sets one
  The Admin page (for anyone given "Manage people and PINs" — the Admin always):
  · People: each person’s role, PIN (set, temporary or not yet; Reset gives a new temporary PIN, shown once), Can sign in, their pages and what they can do. They get their role’s pages and actions; anything changed for one person alone gets a dot, and "Back to the role" undoes it
  · Add a person: name and role — a temporary PIN they change at their first sign-in
  · Someone leaves: turn off their sign-in — their punches, notes and ROs keep their name
  · Roles: one table — every page and action for every role; changing a role changes everyone in it, except what was changed for one person
  · Sign-in rules: the end-of-day sign-out, and a lock when nobody’s touched it (never, 15, 30 or 60 minutes)
  · Sign-in log: sign-ins, switches, locks, wrong PINs and permission changes, by day
  · Manager → Team goes through the same: role, pages, a PIN reset, adding and turning off people
  What the permissions hold, everywhere:
  · Open new ROs · Change an RO’s status · Release a truck · Reopen a completed RO · Delete ROs · Start and stop their own clock · Fix their own punches · Fix anyone’s punches · Move time to someone else · Approve timesheets · Order parts · Adjust stock counts · See costs and prices · File warranty claims · Manage people and PINs · Change settings and automations · Export or reset the data
  · A button you can’t use is hidden; if you get to it anyway it says "You can’t delete ROs — Tom or the Admin can change that on the Admin page" and nothing changes. Automations and the Admin are never stopped
  · Stopping your own clock is always allowed. Without "See costs and prices", money shows as —

Parsec 12.9 —an RO status you pick holds; reopening a completed RO asks about its operations
  · The fix: the Status sync (the RO follows its operations) overrode a status picked by hand at the next thing that happened on the RO — a part, a note, a road-test edit — so a completed RO set back to Hold tech, Working or Waiting snapped back to Complete. Now a status you pick holds until the operations add up to something new: a clock starts, an operation goes on hold, it's completed again
  · Setting a completed, released or closed RO back to an open status — on the RO page, the RO List, Triage, or the Technician page — asks: "RO 1234 is open again — it still has 2 operations marked Complete." Reopen both operations (Enter) puts them back to Hold tech so the clock can start; Just the RO (Esc) leaves them complete. Several ROs set at once ask once
  · The RO comes off the closed list when it's set back to open, whoever closed it
  · The Technician page shows the RO's own status ("RO · Complete – Released ▾") with a menu of RO statuses, next to the operation's state
  · An operation's status picked on the RO page stands like one picked anywhere else (Waiting – Parts no longer turns into Hold tech once the parts are in)
  · Each step is its own Undo

Parsec 12.8 —a completed operation opens back up: its status and its clock
  · The clock card (the RO page and the Technician page): the green check on a completed operation is a button — point at it and it turns into Start ("Start again"); a click (or Enter) reopens the operation and starts your clock, with the start reason as usual
  · Reopen, beside the Done badge: back to Hold tech with the clock off, for when it isn't your turn on it yet
  · The status pill: on the RO page, picking any status but Complete reopens it ("Op 1 reopened — Waiting – Customer"); on the Technician page a completed operation's pill opens too — Start the clock again, Reopen — back in Next, or straight onto a hold
  · The Technician page: the operation row's clock button and the big time's play button stay on a completed operation (Start again), and the done RO's panel has Start again next to Reopen
  · Starting a clock on a completed operation anywhere reopens it — Triage's done toggle and a failed road test too
  · On the record: each reopening keeps when and who (the card shows "Reopened 3:32 pm · Mike" for three days), goes in the audit log, and an RO that was completed, released or closed opens again with it
  · One Undo takes it all back

Parsec 12.7 —each person keeps their own clocks, the lists fold, change a clock while it runs
  Each person keeps their own clocks:
  · Your job clock and your day clock are yours: switching who's signed in, or signing out, never stops anyone else's clock — "Your clock keeps running on RO 4398"
  · Starting a job stops only your own running job. Two people can be on the clock at once, on two operations or on the same one
  · The clock card: Who's on the clock shows everyone, and the card breathes while anyone's on it; Start / Stop, hold to stop, splits, notes and fixing the start are about your own clock ("Start your clock on this operation too"). The RO page's band button and setting an operation's status stop only your own clock
  · The sign-in screen lists every running clock — whose, which RO, since when
  · Lunch ends the day clock of the person who stopped for it, and only theirs — right away (it used to wait for the next save); the lunch pause and the end of day stop only your own clock
  · Setting an operation's status by hand on Triage (done, on hold) stops everyone's clock on it, not just the first one
  · Overlaps are per person: your time can't overlap your own, but it can overlap someone else's
  · Your day's numbers (the day ribbon, your totals, the warnings) count only your punches and day clocks. Older day clocks that never said whose belong to the one person whose punches are in them
  The lists fold:
  · Every page's list folds: the « button in the list's head (Needs action on Triage, Foreman, Parts and Warranty, the Technician page's repair orders, Fixes, the Manager's lists…) folds it into a thin strip with its title, its count and how many are late, and the page's main pane gets the whole width. A click on the strip, or ], opens it again
  · Each group in a list (Late, Arrived, Road test, Free techs, Working…) opens and closes on its own with a click on its head; the count stays in sight, and the row you have open stays visible under a folded group
  · Both are remembered per page on this computer. On a phone the list is its own screen, so only the groups fold there
  Change a clock while it runs:
  · Its start: on both Time tabs as before, and now in the Time Log — the running row has the pencil, E and double-click like every other row
  · Stop it at an earlier time: the running punch's "now" is a button. Type when it really stopped and it asks why with the stop reasons; it stops then, its time goes on the job's burnt hours, and it's marked edited. Never in the future or before the start; past midnight works. In the Time Log, type a Stop for the running punch and Stopped as appears
  · Its reason: the start reason changes while it runs (both Time tabs and the Time Log)
  · Move it to another operation: the ⇄ tool on the running punch in both Time tabs, and the Operation picker in the Time Log — this RO's operations, the person's other open ROs, or shop time. It keeps its start; the clock card, the sidebar clock and Who's on the clock follow it
  · The day clock's start while it's on: the pencil on the Time Log's Clocked in row and beside "On the clock since 07:02" in the clock pop-up. Not in the future, not after the first punch, not before the clock-out before it; changing someone else's time asks why
  · Every change is one Undo (an automation that follows a change goes back with it, however many steps), saves, and redraws both pages
  Also: on a phone, tapping a day in the Time Log opens it; the Time Log's "day, in order" fits a phone

Parsec 12.6 — parts stages on the Parts and Foreman pages
  · The RO section on the Parts page and on the Foreman page has the parts stage below the details, in steps like the Warranty page: To order · Ordered · Received · Staged · Picked up · Installed, each step in its part status color
  · The part that's furthest behind sets the stage. A backorder is part of Ordered — the step turns red and pulses. Parts on operations still waiting for the customer's approval don't count (the head says how many wait)
  · Under each step: how many parts are there, their ETA, and how long the oldest has sat there — amber at 2 days, red at 5
  · The head says the stage, how many parts are in hand, and when they'll all be in: "All in by Fri", "No date yet — not ordered", "Due Tue — late", "All in hand", "All installed"
  · The part that holds the stage is named under the track ("Held by Sensor gasket (Op 1) · Ordered · ETA tomorrow · 3 d") and highlighted in the Parts page's parts list
  · Click a step to show only its parts (the Parts page's list filters, Show all brings them back; the Foreman page lists them under the track). Point at a step for its parts
  · The current step carries its action — on the Parts page Order… / Pull…, Chase…, It's in, Stage it, Tell the tech (a note to the RO's tech that the parts are ready, and the bin); on the Foreman page Ask Parts… / Chase with Parts…, and Tell the tech once they're in
  · Each operation gets its own mini stage — on the Parts page's operation cards and in the Foreman's ops strip
  · The Parts page's Needs action rows carry a small stage pill
  · An RO with no parts says so in one quiet line
