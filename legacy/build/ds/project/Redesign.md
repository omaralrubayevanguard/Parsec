# RO page — what changed

The numbers match the markers on the ROPage pattern and the walkthrough mock-up. Operations now open on their Parts tab.

| # | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ten uppercase tabs across the top, plus search, clock, bell, settings and profile icons on the right | A left **Sidebar** in three groups (top · Shop · You), filtered by role; search (⌘K), clock-in and the inbox live in it; collapses to icons |
| 2 | No sense of where the RO sits in the list | A 48px **PageHeader**: breadcrumb, previous / next (K / J) with “4 of 12”, quiet icon actions, ⋯ options |
| 3 | RO number as the headline; customer hidden behind “Show all 17” | Customer in the serif title, RO number · opened · days open above it, vehicle · unit · bay below, then the status button and who has it |
| 4 | Seven unlabelled dashes | A labelled **StageTrack** with one summary line and the ready-about forecast; each step opens a checkbox popover |
| 5 | “Next step” text with two equal buttons, far from the status | A **NextStep** card at the top of the RO details pane, above Schedule, the only tinted block; its actions move it on to the next step |
| 6 | Operation cards in a side column, the work on the right | **OperationList** rows like sub-tasks; the selected operation opens in place |
| 7 | A long progress bar with the timer floating in it | A **TimeCard**: big tabular timer, thin bar with a quoted tick, Quoted · Burnt · Left · Efficiency, one round play / stop |
| 8 | Four key facts, “Show all 17”, a row of count chips | A **PropertyList** in the RO details pane: Schedule · People · Vehicle · Paperwork · Money as collapsible groups with summaries; the pane never scrolls — opening a group folds the oldest if needed |
| 9 | History behind a count chip | An **ActivityFeed** on the page, with notes and a composer |
| 10 | Every status and reason shown at once in one tall menu | A searchable **StatusMenu**: nine statuses with shortcut numbers, reasons in a second level |
| 11 | Part status as fixed text | A **PartStatus** dropdown per part: five statuses (1–5), edit, copy number, move to another operation, remove |
| 12 | Modals styled one by one | One **Modal** pattern for Transfer, Add operation and Delete: scrim with blur, dialog rises 8px, Esc / ⌘↵, focus managed |
| 13 | Hours, parts and files counted one operation at a time, inside each operation | **HeaderTotals** top right: Time `6.27 / 11.90 h`, Parts `3 / 5`, Files `6`, live; each opens a **TotalsModal** with the breakdown by operation, and a click on a row jumps to that operation |
| 14 | The clock-in card shows only the shift | The clock-in card also shows the **running job** and its time, with a stop button; tap it to jump there. The phone's **ClockBar** is its twin |

Phone: **Parsec Mobile** rebuilt on the same system — see PhoneApp, ClockBar and DayStrip, and the Phone section of the brand book.

Also: uppercase labels become sentence case; coloured filled status pills become icon + ink word; the colourful icon set gives way to the Parsec stroke set; Poppins and IBM Plex give way to Geist, Geist Mono and Newsreader; light and dark are designed as equals.
