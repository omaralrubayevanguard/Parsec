Day reportPick A, B or C. Mike’s Thursday; the numbers are made up but add up. Nothing is saved.

  • A · One sheetThe Timesheet’s paper, for one tech’s day: totals, the day on one line, jobs, every punch in order, checks, signatures. The name picker switches tech or Whole shop.
  • B · Review list + sheetSame sheet, with the day’s techs on the left — status, flags, Approve the day. Built for the foreman’s morning sign-off. J / K to move.
  • C · Whole shopEvery tech on one page: a line per tech, jobs worked across the shop, everything to look at. Replaces the End-of-day sheet pop-up.
  • Print viewExactly what prints — no sidebar, no buttons. P toggles it, Esc goes back.
  • My pickA with C behind the picker — one page, one print button. Add B’s list later if the foreman wants to sign days from here instead of the Timesheet.
