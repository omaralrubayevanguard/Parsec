Parsec · Foreman — QC & Handoff

QC & HandoffBuilds on every Foreman change so far. Use the buttons above, or press 4 for Tomorrow.

  • 1 · QC before releaseAfter the road test, the truck comes to you for QC. Parsec checks what it can — every op closed, a story on each op, hours against the quote, the road test result, parts used or to return. You tick the walk-around. Pass sends it to Dana under Ready to go; Send back puts it at the top of the tech’s queue with your reason.
  • 2 · Foreman badgeThe Foreman icon carries a count of what needs you — red when something is late — on every page, and in the browser tab. Click it for the top items. Choose which groups count.
  • 3 · Tomorrow’s boardA fourth view next to Floor · Techs · Yard. Booked appointments, work that won’t finish today, parts due tomorrow and maybe-approvals, laid out by tech with each one’s hours. Whoever’s off tomorrow is left out. Plan the rest fills by skill and hours; drag or click to move. Each tech’s #1 shows on his Console at clock-in.
  • 3 · Handoff noteWritten for you from the board — who’s off, what has no tech, where each tech starts, which bays stay blocked. Edit it and send it for 07:00; it shows on every Console and Dana’s Triage.
