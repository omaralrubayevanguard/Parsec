  1. 1
    Sidebar replaces the ten top tabs

    Grouped by job, filtered by role, collapsible. Search, clock-in and the inbox live here.

  2. 2
    A header that knows where you are

    Breadcrumb, previous / next RO (K / J), “4 of 12”, quiet actions.

  3. 3
    The customer is the headline

    Serif name; RO number and age above; vehicle · unit · bay below; status and owner under.

  4. 4
    Stages with names

    Seven labelled steps and one summary line. Click a step for its checklist.

  5. 5
    Next lives in the details pane

    Top of the pane, above Schedule. Press Estimate sent and watch it move on to the next step.

  6. 6
    Operations as rows

    Status, type, hours, efficiency, owner. The selected one opens in place.

  7. 7
    A calm clock

    Big timer, quoted tick, four numbers, one round play / stop.

  8. 8
    Details pane fits — no scrolling

    Five collapsible groups; closed ones show a summary. Opening one closes the oldest if space runs out.

  9. 9
    History on the page

    Activity and notes in one timeline.

  10. 10
    A status menu you can type into

    Nine statuses, 1–9 shortcuts, reasons in a second level. Press S.

  11. 11
    Parts dropdown

    Click a part's status: set Not ordered → Installed (1–5), edit, copy the number, move it to another operation, or remove it.

  12. 12
    Modals

    Open from a link, a menu item or a key (T, O). Scrim fades in, the dialog rises 8 px; Esc, Cancel or a click outside closes; ⌘↵ confirms.

  13. 13
    Totals in the header

    Time (burnt / quoted), parts (in hand / total) and files, top right. They update live; click one for its breakdown, then click a row to jump to that operation.

  14. 14
    Running job in the sidebar

    Start a clock and the clock-in card shows the job and its time; tap it to jump there, or stop it from the card.

  15. ·
    Micro-interactions

    Groups fold with an ease, tab underline slides, status and part icons pop in, a copied VIN ticks, a changed row glows bronze for a moment, the clock dot breathes. All of it switches off under “reduce motion”.
