Parsec · Foreman — Triage patterns carried over

ForemanTry the buttons above, or the keys. Nothing here is saved.

  • Needs action, always on the leftWhat’s waiting on the foreman, in groups: Sent to you · Waiting on you · Needs a tech · Bring in · Road test · Watch. Same keys as Triage: J K, ↵, S snooze, A assign, T transfer. Snoozed and Cleared today at the bottom.
  • Facts are filters“2 waiting on you”, “3 need a tech” and “3 late” narrow the list. “22 on the yard” clears it.
  • Floor · Techs · YardThe selected RO three ways. Floor is the bay board with a live clock, nobody-on time and hours used against the quote. Techs is one lane per tech with their load against the hours left today. Yard is the Triage yard.
  • DecideFor Waiting – Foreman. Every choice is a transfer with a reason — back to the tech, warranty, approval or parts — or “I’ll look at it”, which snoozes it with you.
  • Assign is tech and bay in oneTechs sorted lightest first, with their hours left. Pick a bay in the same step. An arrival gets a tech and a bay before it lands.
  • Bring in · Road test · WatchApproved trucks with parts in hand wait for a bay. Finished ops wait for a road test. Watch holds what’s held too long, over the quote, or two trucks on one spot.
  • DragA list item, a Waiting-for-a-bay chip or a card onto an open bay, a lot spot or another tech’s lane. Undo on every toast.
