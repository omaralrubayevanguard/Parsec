What changes

  • Misreads get caughtParsec checks what it reads: the VIN's check digit, hours against the labor price, parts against the totals. Anything it isn't sure of is marked, and Check shows the spot on the screenshot beside the value.Point at any value to see where it came from.
  • A ticket, not a long formRO, customer and status first, then the truck, dates and shop in short rows. Empty fields stay hidden until you add one.
  • No colored rowsPlain paper and thin lines. A small amber mark only where something needs you.
  • Files in a slim stripA thumbnail and a short name across the top. Point at one to light up what it gave.

Where it opens

  • The New RO buttonStays where it is. Opens empty, ready for a paste.
  • Drop or paste anywhereDrag files onto any page, or Ctrl V outside a text box. It opens with them already reading.
  • From an open ROImport, Extract operations and Extract parts open the same window, aimed at that RO. A case whose RO number is already in Parsec switches to Update on its own.
  • Library and InventoryAdd document and Import a parts list open it too, aimed there.
