ManagerTry the buttons above, or the keys. Nothing here is saved; the numbers are made up but add up.

  • Calendar periodsToday, this week or last week, this month or last month, this quarter or last quarter, and this year. The week starts Monday; “this” runs to today and compares with the same days before. It opens on This month.
  • The charts follow the calendarA week shows Monday to Saturday, a month its days, a quarter its weeks, a year its months — the days still to come sit empty.
  • Manager onlyNobody else can open the page — Team shows it locked for every other role, and the lock by the title says so.
  • 12 tilesThe Overview keeps 12 spots. Take one off and its spot waits for another; “The usual 12” puts them back.
