ConsoleTry the buttons above, or the keys. Nothing here is saved.

  • Same panel as the other pagesHeader facts, view tabs (Today · Shop), your work on the left and the job on the right, keys, toasts with Undo.
  • Your workOne list: the job you’re on, Frank’s order for today, what’s waiting and why, what you finished — replaces the Punch On deck, the order card and the second timer card.
  • The job cardQuoted against burnt with the notch at the quote and the overrun in the severity color, Remaining in h:mm — the RO page rules. Hold, Switch, Stop and Done right there.
  • Waiting says why and whoParts, approval, tech support or a second opinion — with the person who has it and how long. Ask them in one click, the way Triage and Foreman hand things off.
  • Parts statusesOrdered, Staged at the counter, Picked up — from the Parts page. Picked up tells Ben.
  • Use fixA matching past fix shows on the job, and only goes in when you press Use fix — the Fixes rule.
  • Your dayThe Time Log bar — on a job, air, shop time, lunch, the shift end and now — with today’s hours sold against your goal (Career) and anything to fix in the Time Log.
  • ShopThe team right now and every open RO by stage, Mine or All. Frank and Ray land here; the Foreman and Manager pages stay where the real work is.
  • What went awayThe greeting, the bento cards, the calendar, the truck pictures and Recent changes. Reminders like the backup go to the Inbox.
