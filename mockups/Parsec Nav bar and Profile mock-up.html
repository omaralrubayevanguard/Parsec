Nav bar & ProfileTry the buttons above, or the keys. Nothing here is saved.

  • One axisEvery icon sits in the same 36 × 36 box, centered on one line down the rail — logo, search, clock, pages and you. Nothing is boxed differently, nothing is offset.
  • Nothing moves when it opensGroup labels and the short dividers take the same 24 px, the clock and you take 44 px either way — open the sidebar and the icons stay exactly where they were.
  • The clockOne light: green on a job, teal on air, purple on shop time, grey when out. Open, it reads On the clock · 6:47 and the job underneath. Click it for Stop, Lunch, End of day.
  • You, at the bottomRecommended: the avatar where the gear was — your tools at the top, you at the bottom, where Claude and Linear keep it. The gear goes away. Below the clock is the other option, in the toolbar.
  • Profile menuTheme and size right there, then Settings, Your data, Keyboard shortcuts, Switch user and Log out. The backup warning rides on the avatar.
  • Profile pageYou, Sign in & out, Appearance, Your data, Work tools, Shortcuts — and Admin for Ray. Everything the settings menu held, with room to read.
  • Log out ≠ clock outLogging out leaves your clock running and says so. Optional: log out when you clock out for the day, lock after 15 minutes, a PIN.
  • Moved out of settingsNew repair order goes to the RO List header, Triage check-in and Ctrl K.
