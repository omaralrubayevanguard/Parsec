InboxTry the buttons above, or the keys. Nothing here is saved.

  • What reaches youPer page: phone and inbox, inbox only, or off. Hand-offs and asks always reach you. Quiet after 6 pm.
  • Replaces the bellThe warnings and reminders in the bell today come here as Reminders — clocks left running, parts past ETA, no punch in days.
