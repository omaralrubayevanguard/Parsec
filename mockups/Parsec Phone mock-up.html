Parsec · Phone — technicians

PhoneEvery phone works — tap around. Mike’s Friday; nothing is saved.

  • Technicians onlySigning in as a technician opens the phone view. Three places: Work, Inbox, Fixes. Triage, Foreman, Manager, Parts, the RO list and Settings stay on the computer.
  • View and changeTap a job: notes (type or talk, Enter adds the time), parts, files, cause and correction. The status chip changes the status.
  • ClockClock in on Start of day; Clock out has Lunch, a break, and End of day — which checks what’s still open first.
  • StatusStop opens the same choices as the computer: Switched job, Waiting – Parts / Approval / Foreman / Tech Support, Complete (needs the correction).
  • AttachCamera, photos, scan or a file — filed under Dash pic, Pictures, DTCs or Other, saved with the RO.
  • A or BA: tabs at the bottom. B: one Work screen, Inbox and Fixes as buttons at the top — more room for jobs.
  • LaterGetting the phone’s changes into the shop folder (sync) comes after you pick the look.
