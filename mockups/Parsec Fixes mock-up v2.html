Fixes · v2Your four decisions. Everything from the first mock-up still works. Nothing here is saved.

  • By system or flatThe complaints list groups by system or runs as one flat list — either way, sort by most fixed, recent or A–Z.
  • Use fix is a buttonNothing fills in by itself. Use fix shows the quote and parts it will put on the RO, and changes nothing until you press it. Open ROs shows who used it.
  • Who reviewsForeman, lead tech, Triage and the manager. Any of them can clear an item, and it shows who did. Technicians don’t see Review — what they add or suggest goes there.
  • HeldUnchanged: a fix held when the truck didn’t come back for the same complaint within 30 days.
