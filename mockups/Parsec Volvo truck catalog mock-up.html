Truck catalogEvery Volvo model, generation and configuration that comes through the shop, and the picture each one needs.

  • The drawingsEach card is a generic drawing of that configuration: cab, sleeper, roof, hood length, front axle and rear axles, drawn to one scale. No image generation here, and the drawings don't copy Volvo's styling or badge.
  • Real picturesGet official photos from Volvo's dealer media (ask your Volvo rep for the brand portal), or take your own. Add each one in Your data › Truck pictures with its model and years. One picture covers every model year in its range.
  • Your four photosThese are already in their slots: VNL 760, VNL 860, VNR 300 and VHD. The blue and red labels were guesses, so fix them in Truck pictures if they're wrong.
  • MatchingParsec matches the RO's model (VNL 760) and gets the year from the VIN. The full model code (VNL64T760) also tells the axles and the front axle. Try one at the top.
  • Trims and axlesTrims on the new VNL (Core, Edge, Edge Black, Ultimate) change the finish, not the shape. Axle choices change the rear of the truck. One picture per model is enough unless you want more.
  • What's in itVolvo Trucks North America from 1996 to today: VNL, VNR, VNX, VHD, VAH, VNM and VT. Sources are Volvo and the trade press, October 2026. A ? means it isn't confirmed.
