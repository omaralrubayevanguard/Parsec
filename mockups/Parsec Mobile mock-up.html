Parsec Mobile · technician mock-up

Visualization

  1. 1
    Running clock bar

    Follows you on every screen. Tap it to go back to the job; stop without hunting.

  2. 2
    Day strip

    Today's clocked time as segments per job; gaps are time not on a job. Feeds EOD.

  3. 3
    Jobs, not ROs

    Today lists your operations across every RO — ready vs waiting — with play on each row.

  4. 4
    Stage in one line

    Seven ticks and the current stage instead of the labelled track.

  5. 5
    Totals as tiles

    Time · Parts · Files, same as the desktop header; each opens a bottom sheet.

Efficiency

  1. 6
    Thumb zone

    Start / stop clock sits bottom right — one thumb, no reaching.

  2. 7
    Bottom sheets

    Replace menus and modals: 52px rows, tap outside or swipe down to close.

  3. 8
    Camera first

    Photos land in the operation's Files and the Dash pic / DTC bulletins, shrunk on capture.

  4. 9
    Voice notes

    Hold Note to dictate work performed; the time stamp is added like Enter does today.

  5. 10
    QR sync, upgraded

    Progress ring, what's going, last synced; works with no network, like today.

  6. 11
    Swipe actions

    Swipe an operation to start its clock, a part to mark it Received; long-press for the full menu.

Styling

  1. ·
    Bay mode

    Bigger type and targets, stronger labels — for gloves and bright bays. Try the toggle.

  2. ·
    Phone rules

    16px inputs (no zoom on focus), safe areas, 44px+ targets, tabular numbers, same tokens.

  3. ·
    Follows the phone

    Ivory by day, Graphite when the phone is in dark mode; status colours never change.
