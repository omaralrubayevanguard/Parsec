CareerTry the buttons above, or the keys. Nothing here is saved.

  • 1 · Why it movedNext to the efficiency chart: what each kind of job added to the change, in points (or hours sold), and the one job that did the most — it opens in Work.
  • 2 · Quote feedbackGrowth tab. Your time against the quote, job by job, this year — each dot a job. Where quotes run tight, Tell Frank sends it to him with the numbers.
  • 3 · Your bestsBest week, most hours in a week, best month, weeks in a row at goal, days without a comeback — with this week and month underneath.
  • 4 · Same days last yearCompare with the period before or the same days last year. Tiles, the chart and Why it moved all follow. C switches it.
  • 5 · Coaching notesFrom Frank, dated, with his goal and how you’re tracking. Got it lets him know you saw it; Reply goes to his Inbox.
  • 6 · Skills & trainingCertificates with expiry, what’s in progress, the next class. Frank uses it when he hands out work.
  • 7 · Fixes you wroteEach fix you wrote in Fixes — who used it, how often it held, the hours it saved others.
  • 8 · Pay estimateFlat rate for this pay period: flagged so far, the rest at your pace, and at your goal. Amounts stay hidden until you show them.
  • 9 · Goal alertsUnder your goals: an Inbox note when you fall behind pace, a Friday recap, a new best. Only you get them.
  • 10 · StatementOne printable page — your numbers, month by month, bests, certificates, fixes. Never pay or coaching notes.
  • Who sees thisThe Private chip in the header: you and Ray see everything, Frank sees all but pay, other techs see nothing.
  • Earlier yearsAdd your years before Parsec from old time sheets. They show as “by hand” next to Parsec’s numbers — switch between the two.
