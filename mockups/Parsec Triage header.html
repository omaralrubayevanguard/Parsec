HeaderUse the buttons above the frame. For the badge, open another page, then press +30 min twice.

  • 1 · Facts are filtersClick “4 late”, “4 awaiting approval” or “3 ready to release” to narrow Needs action — and dim everything else on the Yard. Click it again, the chip in the list, or Esc to clear. “17 on the yard” shows everything. No tabs.
  • 2 · Late badge on the railOn any other page, the Triage icon shows how many ROs are late. When one turns late while you’re away, the badge pulses and a note says which. Back on Triage, those ROs carry a New tag until you open them.
  • 3 · Search is “where’s my truck?”Search by RO, unit, customer, the last digits of the VIN, or a spot (“B2”). Each result shows where it is: zone, spot and key tag — or the ETA for an arrival. ⇧↵ or the pin jumps to the Yard with that spot lit. Released trucks show when they left.
  • In ParsecLate uses the app’s own late rules (tyLate) plus overdue arrivals and missed back-by times. The badge is the same count as the late fact. Search reads the same RO, unit, VIN and location fields the old “Where’s my truck?” used.
