Truck picturesSample ROs, made-up numbers. The pictures are the ones you sent.

  • How it picksFrom the VIN: year and model (VNL 760, VNR 300, Mack Pinnacle …), sleeper or day cab, and the unit’s color when it’s set at check-in. It’s there the moment the RO is made.
  • Model pictureOne picture per model and color, kept with Parsec. The shop supplies them — from Volvo’s dealer media or your own photos — so the pictures are ones you’re allowed to use.
  • Check-in photoTriage takes a side photo of the actual truck at check-in (next to the dash picture). It replaces the model picture on that RO — dents, decals and all.
  • No picture yetA plain outline of a truck until a picture for that model is added.
