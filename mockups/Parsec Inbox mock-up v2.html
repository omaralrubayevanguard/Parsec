InboxTry the buttons above, or the keys. Nothing here is saved.

  • 1 · One thread per ROEverything about the same truck is one row with its count; the detail shows each one with its own actions.
  • 2 · Snooze untilA time, or until the parts arrive, the customer replies or the RO moves — then it comes back on top.
  • 3 · Clears itselfDo it on its own page and the inbox item closes on its own — Done shows “cleared itself”.
  • 4 · Reminder, then backupAn ask past its hold goal reminds you, then goes to your backup too (Frank → Mike, lead tech).
  • 5 · AwayPick who covers your asks and until when; they show up in the cover’s inbox under “Covering for”.
  • 6 · SeenSent shows what you handed off — sent, seen, done — with Nudge when it isn’t seen.
  • 7 · Morning summaryAt clock-in, here and on the phone: what needs you, what’s due, clocks still running, what was passed on.
  • 8 · PhoneAsks buzz the phone; FYIs wait. Asks can’t be turned off. Quiet after 6 pm.
  • 9 · Pick severalPick rows (X) and clear them at once — done, snooze or read.
  • 10 · End of shiftPass what’s still open to the next person, with a note that lands in their morning summary.
  • Kept personalOnly what’s sent to you; shop-wide activity stays on the Activity log. Every item opens its RO — or its page.
